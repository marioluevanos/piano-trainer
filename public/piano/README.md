# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, recorded from a Yamaha C5.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

These MP3s are the subset published by the Tone.js project
(https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/): A0, C/D#/F#/A for octaves 1-7, and C8.
`src/midi/synth.ts` pitch-shifts the nearest sample to fill the notes in between.
